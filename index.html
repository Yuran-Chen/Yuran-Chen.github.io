About Me

I am Rainy (Yuran) Chen. I have been admitted to the Master of Finance (M.Fin.) program at Fudan University, with a concentration in Financial Technology, and expect to enroll in 2027. I am currently completing my B.S. in Financial Technology at Dongbei University of Finance and Economics (DUFE), where I rank 1/58 with a GPA of 4.09/5.00.

I remain open to research collaboration and research internships, especially on trustworthy LLM behavior, evaluation, explainable AI, and AI-driven decision systems.

🔬Research agenda

Research

“I aim to build trustworthy AI systems that preserve independent judgment, diagnose failure modes, quantify uncertainty, and support reliable real-world decisions.”

My work combines controlled evaluation, Bayesian deep learning, explainable AI, and hybrid neural architectures to study LLM reliability, anomaly detection, and robust forecasting under distribution shift.

01

Trustworthy LLM Behavior & Evaluation

I study how models lose independence under one-sided multi-turn narratives and how hallucinations can be diagnosed by separating memory, reasoning, and instruction-following failures.

02

Uncertainty-Aware AI for Finance & Security

I develop interpretable, uncertainty-aware models for high-stakes settings, including feature screening, Bayesian-XAI anomaly detection, and risk-sensitive decision support.

03

Robust Forecasting & Cross-Domain Modeling

I investigate models that transfer across heterogeneous environments, including multi-scale CNN-Transformer hybrids and competition-grade modeling pipelines.

Publications

2026

  1. EMNLP ’26Accepted

    Caught in the Story: Narrative Captivity in Multi-turn LLMs Conversation

    Yuhe Wu, Guangyu Wang, Yujie Chen, Jiatong Zhang, Yuran Chen, Yutong Zhang, Xiyin Cheng, Wenpeng Cao, Zhuang Liu, Guang Zhang.

    Accepted to EMNLP 2026 · Acceptance announced Aug 2026.

    Introduces narrative captivity, a failure mode in which LLMs progressively align with an unopposed, one-sided account. The study builds a 5,078-scenario benchmark across six moral dimensions, evaluates 17 LLMs, and shows that preference optimization is a major contributor while inference-time interventions offer only partial relief.

    PDF arXiv
  2. ACL ’26Main Conference

    PRISM: Probing Reasoning, Instruction, and Source Memory in LLM Hallucinations

    Yuhe Wu, Guangyu Wang, Yuran Chen, Jiatong Zhang, Yutong Zhang, Yujie Chen, Jiaming Shang, Guang Zhang, Zhuang Liu.

    ACL 2026 Main Conference · Acceptance announced Jul 2026.

    PRISM introduces a stage-aware benchmark with 9,448 instances across 65 tasks to diagnose hallucinations by separating memory, reasoning, and instruction-following failures.
