🚀 Research Thrust

"Building trustworthy AI for finance and security: from uncertainty-aware detection and explainable decisions to robust, generalizable forecasting across domains."

My work combines Bayesian deep learning, explainable AI (XAI), and hybrid neural architectures (e.g., CNN–Transformer) to improve anomaly detection, uncertainty quantification, and cross-region prediction—with applications in cyber risk, air pollution, and LLM fairness.

LLM Safety, Fairness & Explainability

Designing plug-in frameworks that integrate Bayesian inference with XAI to boost detection accuracy while quantifying uncertainty and providing interpretable explanations; building multi-dimensional benchmarks (e.g., POLARIS) for explicit and implicit bias in LLMs.

Robust Forecasting & Cross-Domain Transfer

Developing multi-scale hybrid models (e.g., PolluVCCT) for robust forecasting across diverse climate zones; applying similar ideas to financial and behavioral data (e.g., Kaggle MABe challenge) with careful feature engineering and post-processing.
